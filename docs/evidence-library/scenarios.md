# Scenarios & Forecasts

**Status:** distinguish *historical reported events* and *illustrative* timelines from speculative lunar development forecasts.

## Core challenge scenario: landing date slips
1. Define site A, original landing date, surface operating duration and hard limits (darkness, DTE geometric blackout, slope).
2. Compare site A at a delayed landing date. Keep date intervals and altitude assumptions consistent.
3. If any threshold fails, compare site B or an alternative date.
4. Show the changed mission **margin**, not just ranking. Report unknowns distinctly.
5. Save/describe inputs for reproducibility. Do not claim the app can negotiate replacement rockets or guarantee a landing window.

### Scenario definitions
- **A / Baseline:** Site A, date T, surface operations D days.
- **B / Delay:** Site A, date T+14 days, operations D.
- **C / Alternative:** nearby site B, date T+14 days, operations D.
All inputs are illustrative until they are associated with a saved simulation and its output. No scenario outcome is asserted here.

## Forecasts for 2027–2032 (not predictions)
| Scenario | Assumptions | Implication for Lunar Margin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slow deployment | deliveries slip and no dependable surface infrastructure | robust standalone Sun/Earth geometry and alternative date search |
| Gradual deployment | selected locations gain limited relay/power access | site/date comparisons plus optional, validated infrastructure inputs |
| Faster deployment | multiple colocated activities and shared facilities | multi-mission temporal conflict and service-access layers |

Do not model imaginary relay coverage as available today. Distinguish *mission announcements*, *contracts*, *operational assets*, and *aspirational roadmaps*.

## Useful historical evidence topics (verify the primary source before publication)
- Commercial Lunar Payload Services schedule changes and landing-site changes: NASA OIG CLPS audit.
- Lunar polar lighting-constrained delayed missions: use source-grounded reporting; do not assume exact window geometry without flight-plan data.
- Airline delay analogy: use it as a narrative, not as scientific evidence.
- Artemis candidate-region maps: historical 2022 versus later selections must be labeled with dates.

## Limits
Sun/Earth geometry is only a subset of launch-window and landing feasibility. Earth–Moon transfer dynamics, vehicle safety envelopes, operational comms links and real power balance require additional verified models.
